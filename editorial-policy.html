Editorial policy

How we maintain learning content

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Accuracy and review

The Commerce Study Connect Editorial Team organizes and reviews educational material for clarity and usefulness. We distinguish practice content from official guidance and encourage learners to verify current rules, syllabi, and announcements with the relevant authority.

Corrections and updates

When a factual issue or outdated explanation is identified, we investigate it, update the affected content, and revise the page date. Send corrections and supporting sources to commercestudyconnect@gmail.com.

Independence

This is an independent online learning resource. It is not affiliated with ICAI, ICMAI, MCA, or any other examination authority. Course pages and quizzes are for study support and are not professional, legal, tax, or financial advice.
