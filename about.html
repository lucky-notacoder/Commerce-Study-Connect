What we do

Commerce Study Connect is an online learning resource for students preparing for commerce qualifications. We organize course notes, practice questions, quizzes, and progress tools in one place.

Our editorial team

Content is published and maintained by the Commerce Study Connect Editorial Team. We review explanations for clarity, identify gaps in source material, and update pages when content changes. This site is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with a professional institute or government body.

Questions, corrections, and source suggestions can be sent to commercestudyconnect@gmail.com.

Reference sources

For primary rules, syllabus details, and official announcements, consult the relevant authority:

  • Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)

Online-only support

Commerce Study Connect operates online and provides support by email. We do not claim a public physical office address. For support or correction requests, visit our Contact page.
